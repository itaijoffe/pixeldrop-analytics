Key Findings

Business Insights

Consistent findings validated across all three tools — SQL, Python and Power BI.

Revenue & Growth
$6M total (2023–2024). Sneakers dominate revenue (36%) but Accessories deliver the best gross margin (65%). A ~2.5% dip in 2024 was flagged for executive review.
Product Strategy
Revenue leaders and volume leaders are completely different lists. Only Retro Runner Sneaker appears in both top 5 — making it the one true sweet-spot product in the range.
Customer Loyalty
79% repeat customer rate and 91% retention YoY. RFM identifies 406 Champions (target for loyalty perks) and 343 At-Risk customers (target for win-back campaigns).
Returns
7.69% overall — well below the 20–30% industry average. Air Hoodie has the highest product return rate (7.31%), suggesting a sizing issue. Two serial returners identified with ~75% personal return rates — behavioural, not a product defect.
Discount Risk
54.5% of line items had discounts applied. Correlation analysis shows near-zero relationship between discount level and units purchased — the business is giving away margin with no volume benefit.
Geographic Signal
Florida and Georgia show the highest customer return rates. Worth investigating — potential causes include sizing chart gaps or logistics/fulfilment issues in those markets.
